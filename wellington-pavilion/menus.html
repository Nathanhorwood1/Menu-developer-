How the costing works. Each dish lists its main ingredients by weight, priced from the price book. "Everything else" covers sauces, garnish, bread and oil, and it's an estimate. Menu price is divided by 1.15 to remove GST before food cost % is calculated. Sourced prices are mostly published retail or online seafood-merchant prices, so they run higher than a trade account. Your real food cost should come in 10–20% under these numbers once Bidfood, Gilmours or Service Foods quote you. Treat anything amber as a target that still needs a quote.
